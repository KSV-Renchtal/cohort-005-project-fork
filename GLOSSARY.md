# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners, one at a time or as Team Licences. This glossary covers the language around access to courses and getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Platform Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who runs the platform, including deciding Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), the team

**Team Admin**:
A member of a customer's team who buys and manages that team's Team Licences. A Team Admin cannot decide Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own)

### Access

**Enrollment**:
A learner's access to a course. Every Enrollment comes from exactly one source: the learner's own purchase or a Claimed Seat.
_Avoid_: Access, Membership, Subscription

**Team Licence**:
A purchase of several places in one course, made by a Team Admin for their team.
_Avoid_: Bulk purchase, Team purchase

**Unclaimed Seat**:
A place in a Team Licence that no learner has taken yet. It cannot be claimed while its Team Licence has an Open Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Code, Seat (on its own)

**Claimed Seat**:
A place in a Team Licence that a learner has taken, giving them an Enrollment. A Team Licence with any Claimed Seat cannot be refunded.
_Avoid_: Redeemed coupon, Seat (on its own)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
An ask, with a reason, to get back what was paid for a purchase. A learner raises it for their own purchase; a Team Admin raises it for a Team Licence. A Platform Admin approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Platform Admin has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, ends the Enrollment that came from that purchase, and voids any Unclaimed Seats. No further Refund Requests can be raised on that purchase.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has turned down. It has no Refund, and a new Refund Request may be raised.

**Refund**:
The money returned after a Refund Request is approved, to the learner or Team Admin who paid. It is always the full amount paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund whose Refund Request is approved but where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
